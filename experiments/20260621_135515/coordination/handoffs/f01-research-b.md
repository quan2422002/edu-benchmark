# Handoff — f01-research-b

## Scope

Programming education, misconceptions, debugging, algorithm/code feedback and
automated tutoring.

## Result

- 26 candidates screened.
- 8 studies deeply extracted.
- Published ACM/IEEE/journal versions retained where verified.

## Main findings

- Correct repair does not establish useful tutoring.
- Diagnosis, issue localization, next-step guidance, answer concealment and
  readability are separable properties.
- Fabricating a bug in correct work is a critical failure.
- Longer or more fluent explanations are not necessarily more effective.
- Inputs should preserve student work, expected behavior/test evidence and
  environment constraints.

## Limits

Most studies concern English-speaking university learners and executable code.
Transfer to Vietnamese Grade-9 pseudocode, flowcharts and algorithms requires
teacher judgment.

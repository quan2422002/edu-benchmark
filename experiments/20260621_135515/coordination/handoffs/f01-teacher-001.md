- a concise start page;
- separated author and independent-reviewer task cards;

## Required boundaries

- All tasks, criteria and examples remain provisional until independent
  teacher review.
- Authors cannot approve their own samples.
- Scores use `0-5` or `N/A`; a reason is required for `N/A`.
- Critical failures are recorded separately and cannot be offset.
- `conversation_history` is an ordered list of `turn + role + text`.
- `critical_failure_flags` is a list of controlled codes; use `[]` when empty.
- T02, T04 and T07 display a direct-evidence warning.

## Human decisions

Teachers must confirm curriculum fit, criterion-specific anchors, critical
failures, local tools/programming conventions, digital-conduct examples and
the decision to retain, revise or remove each low-evidence task.

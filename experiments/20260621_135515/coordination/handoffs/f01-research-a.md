# Handoff — f01-research-a

## Scope

LLM/AI tutoring benchmarks; feedback, scaffolding, hinting, student-state
diagnosis and multi-turn tutoring.

## Result

- 23 unique candidates screened.
- 10 sources deeply extracted.
- Publisher/proceedings versions replaced preprints when verified.
- Two citation-chaining rounds added student uptake but no new major
  tutor-response task family.

## Main findings

- Problem-solving ability is not a proxy for tutoring quality.
- Recurrent dimensions are correctness, diagnosis, guidance, actionability,
  coherence with history and avoidance of premature answers.
- Generic text-overlap metrics are not suitable primary pedagogical metrics.
- Multi-turn evaluation should consider the learner's subsequent response.
- Teachers repeatedly serve as authors, rubric designers, raters and
  adjudicators.

## Limits

Most evidence is English-language mathematics. Direct evidence for Vietnamese
Grade-9 Informatics, digital products, digital conduct and careers is sparse.

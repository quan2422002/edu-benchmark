# Handoff — f01-research-c

## Scope

Human rubric design and reliability, LLM-as-a-judge validity/bias,
multilingual/low-resource evaluation and middle-school evidence.

## Result

- 24 candidates screened.
- 10 sources deeply extracted.
- Search included OpenAlex result counts and targeted primary-source
  verification.

## Main findings

- Analytic behavior-specific criteria can be reliable when definitions,
  training, double-coding and recalibration are explicit.
- Reliability varies substantially by criterion; a single aggregate can hide
  weak constructs.
- LLM judges exhibit order, verbosity, familiarity, anchoring, prompt and
  language-resource biases.
- Vietnamese automatic judging must be calibrated directly against teachers.
- Existing studies do not validate F01's proposed six-level scale.

## Limits

Middle-school evidence is mostly English mathematics/Earth Science. Vietnamese
evidence is exam-solving rather than tutoring.

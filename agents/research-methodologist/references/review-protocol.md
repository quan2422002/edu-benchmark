# Review protocol

## Before searching

Write down:

- review questions;
- target population, domain, intervention, and outcome concepts;
- source databases and date searched;
- search strings;
- inclusion and exclusion rules;
- screening and extraction owners;
- stopping rule and deadline;
- intended synthesis type.

## Search and screening

1. Save the exact query and result count for every source.
2. Deduplicate by DOI, normalized title, and known preprint/published versions.
3. Screen title/abstract before full text.
4. Record one explicit exclusion reason at full-text stage.
5. Use backward and forward citation chaining for core studies.

## Extraction

Use the evidence schema. Capture a section, page, table, or figure location for important evidence. Keep quotations short and use paraphrases in synthesis.

## Synthesis labels

- `evidence`: directly supported by an extracted source.
- `inference`: reasoned implication that goes beyond a source.
- `open_question`: unresolved issue requiring more research or human judgment.

## Quality controls

- Recheck a sample of extractions against source text.
- Label publication status.
- Report absent reliability evidence rather than assuming reliability.
- Distinguish tutoring behavior, student performance, and learning outcomes.
- Ask expert teachers to review pedagogical implications, not search mechanics.
